# v48 — Research vs content ("who did the thinking?") + explicit rules in the methodology

- New rule: a session is **Analysis & Research** when the goal states research intent (research, compare, investigate, evaluate, synthesize, analyze, benchmark, assess options) **and** — when an action history exists — it gathered from **≥ 3 sources** (searches, web fetches, file/mail/channel/transcript reads, data queries, or ≥ 3 data input files). This holds even when the findings end in a deck or doc.
- Research intent without that evidence falls back to the final-output format (usually Document & content creation). With no action history, the goal decides.
- Research that ends in an email stays Email workflows; research that ends in an app or script is Write or debug code.
- Content verbs (draft, design, format, refresh, polish, redesign, build a deck) keep a session in Document & content creation unless an explicit research verb is present.
- `references/classification-methodology.md` now states the full ordered rule set, the file-type map, the research evidence test and worked examples; `references/methodology.md` and SKILL.md summarize it.
